Tentacle Snake.io – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Tentacle Snake.io (Android package com.tpplay.tentaclesnake, the "Game"), published by TP Play Games Studio ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. It applies to the Game as distributed through Google Play.

1. Summary

We do not collect, receive, store, share or sell any personal or sensitive user data. The Game works fully offline. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases with real money.

2. Information stored only on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves the following data locally on your device:

  • in-game coins and upgrades bought with those coins;
  • your best level and game progress;
  • your sound on/off setting.

This data contains no personal information. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no access to it.

3. Information we collect

None. Specifically, the Game does not:

  • ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details;
  • access your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • collect device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
  • include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software (SDKs);
  • send any data over the internet.

4. How we use and share information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not use it, share it with third parties, sell it, or use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain no data about you. The progress data described in section 2 stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the Game's app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

6. Security

Progress data is kept in the Game's private app storage on your device, which other apps cannot read under Android's standard app sandboxing. No data is transmitted, so none is exposed in transit.

7. Third-party services

The Game is downloaded through Google Play. Google may collect information when you use Google Play, under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the Game collects no personal information at all.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or US state privacy laws (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act) — for example, to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to opt out of its sale. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, delete or sell. You can still contact us with any privacy question using the details below, and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes how information is handled (for example, by adding ads, analytics or online features), we will update this page, change the "Last updated" date above, and update the Game's Google Play listing before that version is released.
